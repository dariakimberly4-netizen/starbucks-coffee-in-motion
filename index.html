02 — The Beginning

EVERY STORY
STARTS SOMEWHERE.

Starbucks began in 1971 at Seattle’s Pike Place Market, first known for whole-bean coffee. The idea grew into something larger: a coffeehouse built around conversation, craft, and community.

07 — Starbucks Philippines

A STORY
THAT FOUND HOME.

Starbucks opened its first Philippine store on December 4, 1997 at the 6750 Ayala Building in Makati. The Philippines became the company’s third market outside North America — bringing together coffee, hospitality, and a strong local culture of connection.
